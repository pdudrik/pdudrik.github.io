Privacy policy

School Hub · last updated 16 October 2026

Who uses it

School Hub is a personal app used only by its owner. It does not have other users, accounts or sign-ups.

What it accesses in Google accounts

  • Gmail (read-only): it reads messages from university senders in the owner's inbox to find school notices such as schedule changes and days off. It never sends, changes or deletes mail.
  • Google Calendar: it creates its own "School Hub" calendar and adds, changes and removes events in that calendar only. It cannot see or change any other calendar.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the owner their own schedule, deadlines and school notices. It is stored privately for the owner, is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Stored Google sign-in tokens and mail contents are encrypted.

School Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. The owner can delete the stored data at any time.
