Privacy policy

School Hub and Business Hub · last updated 13 November 2026

School Hub and Business Hub are personal apps used only by their owner. They run on the owner's own private server at home. Any access they get to the owner's Google account is used only for the purposes below. The data is kept private and is not shared, sold, used for advertising or used to train any model.

School Hub reads the owner's school calendar and school mail to show the owner their own school information, and writes the owner's own deadlines to a calendar the app creates itself.

Business Hub reads (read-only) the owner's business mailbox to find invoice attachments, and stores copies of the owner's own business records in a folder in the owner's Google Drive that the app creates itself; it cannot see any other file in the Drive. Part of those copies is encrypted before it is uploaded, and only the owner holds the key.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and the stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
